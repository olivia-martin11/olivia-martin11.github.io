What this is, and how rows were chosen

This is not a news-hit ranking and it does not start from Furman’s file. v1 was nominated from policy recaps (CA YIMBY, YIMBY Law, Terner, SPUR, Sightline, Texas 2036, Mercatus ADU) plus bill pages. Furman is the enacted-bill census we should filter against later; it omits bills that did not pass (SB 50, Housing Compact, TX SB 673, WA TOD).

Did not pass means the bill never became law — introduced, widely discussed, died. Those rows stay in the default view.

In: statewide or large-city-class rules that constrain local zoning, permitting, environmental review, parking, ADUs, TOD, design review, lot size, HOAs, or housing-element enforcement — plus famous non-enacted bills and NY local/tax items that get confused with preemption.

Out: the other ~90 California housing bills, and tenant law (HSTPA).

Wave 2+ sourcing: a row is added only with an official legislature / session-law / agency URL and a short verbatim quote. Recaps and news can nominate a bill; they cannot be the only proof. If the official page is ambiguous, the row is omitted. v1 CA/WA/NY/TX/OR cards do not yet show those quotes.
